Products & Solutions

AI Education · AI Enterprise · Technology Products — building tailored AI capabilities for Hong Kong's schools and SMEs.

01 · Education AI Solutions

Education AI Solutions

Target Clients：Hong Kong primary & secondary schools, tertiary institutions

Market Background

The Quality Education Fund has reserved HK$2 billion to advance digital education in primary and secondary schools, launching school-based AI education projects. Among this, HK$500 million is allocated to the "AI-Enabling Learning and Teaching" scheme, with successful applicant schools receiving a one-off HK$500,000 grant for AI tool procurement, AI literacy training, and infrastructure upgrades.

Core Solutions

AI On-Premise Deployment

Secure, compliant on-premise AI infrastructure for schools, ensuring student data privacy and cybersecurity while meeting strict data sovereignty requirements

RAG Knowledge Base

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-based proprietary knowledge base integrating school-based teaching materials, lesson plans, exam papers, and more — enabling intelligent teaching assistance and personalized learning recommendations

AI-Assisted Teaching

AI lesson planning tools, smart assignment grading, and learning analytics to enhance teaching efficiency and quality

Administrative Automation

AI-powered automation of school administration including student records, timetable optimization, parent-school communication, and financial processing

02 · Enterprise AI Solutions

Enterprise AI Solutions

Target Clients：Hong Kong SMEs

Market Background

The Hong Kong government has launched an optimized "Digital Transformation Support Pilot Programme" with HK$300 million in funding to support SMEs in adopting market-ready AI and cybersecurity digital solutions. Hong Kong's AI development has evolved from isolated initiatives to systematic deployment, aiming to empower all industries with AI.

Core Solutions

AI On-Premise Deployment

Secure, controllable on-premise AI infrastructure for SMEs, meeting data privacy, compliance, and customization needs without relying on public cloud services

RAG Knowledge Base

RAG-based enterprise knowledge base integrating internal documents, policies, product materials, and client information — enabling smart search, knowledge management, and decision support

Internal AI Applications

Covering a wide range of business scenarios:

MarketingCustomer profiling & targeted promotion
OperationsProcess automation & intelligent scheduling
Customer ServiceSmart chatbots & sentiment analysis
Data AnalyticsTrend forecasting & decision support
